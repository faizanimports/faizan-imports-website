Part Number Help

Toyota part number questions, answered

Ordering by part number avoids the wrong fit. These are the questions buyers ask us most about the genuine Toyota parts above.

How do I know which part number fits my Toyota?

Check the part number stamped on the old part or in your service book, then match it exactly. Two parts can look the same but be different — for example, 90919-01191 (SK20HR11) is not the same part as 90919-01210 (SK20R11). Send your part number on WhatsApp and we confirm fitment before quoting.

Are the Toyota parts supplied by Faizan Imports genuine?

Yes. Every part is genuine and brand new, supplied in manufacturer packaging, and checked against its exact part number before it is offered — no substitutes and no “almost the same”.

Which vehicles do the ball joints 43310-09015 and 43330-09510 fit?

43310-09015 is the upper ball joint assembly and 43330-09510 is the lower ball joint assembly for Toyota Hilux and Fortuner models. Share your vehicle model and year on WhatsApp and we will confirm the correct fitment for your vehicle.

What is the difference between fuel filter part numbers 23390-0L070 and 23390-0E011?

Both are fuel filter element assemblies for Toyota diesel engines such as the Hilux, Fortuner and Innova, but they are different elements for different applications — always order by the exact part number on your vehicle. The V-ribbed belt tensioner 16620-0Y061 is a separate service part that keeps the drive belt at the correct tension.

How do I get a price for a part number?

Tap any part number in the index above, or send the part number and quantity on WhatsApp to +66 926 945 006. Prices are shared on enquiry only, and we confirm availability and fitment first.

Potato Export Help

Potato export questions, answered

Export-grade fresh potatoes from Pakistan to Dubai, the UAE and Gulf buyers — graded, packed and shipped in reefer containers.

Does Faizan Imports export potatoes from Pakistan?

Yes. We export fresh, export-grade potatoes from Pakistan to buyers in Dubai, the UAE and other Gulf countries, as well as international buyers worldwide. Potatoes are graded at source, packed in ventilated export bags and shipped in 40ft reefer containers from Karachi.

How are the potatoes packed and shipped?

Potatoes are graded by size (45mm+) and quality, then packed in mesh or PP bags of 10kg, 25kg or 50kg. Shipments go in 40ft reefer containers from Karachi — about 25–27 tonnes per container — with transit of 2–5 days to Jebel Ali, Dubai.

How can I order potatoes from Faizan Imports?

Send your quantity, variety and destination on WhatsApp to +66 926 945 006, or email info@faizanimports.com. Prices are shared on enquiry only, and we confirm availability first.

What is the minimum order for potato export from Pakistan?

Export orders start from one full container — a 20ft container for a first trial order, or a 40ft reefer container for regular buyers in Dubai, the UAE and the Gulf. Send your quantity and destination on WhatsApp and we confirm the container plan and availability first.

When are Pakistani potatoes available for export?

The main autumn crop is harvested in December–February, with the export window running January–May; cold-store supply extends availability beyond the harvest season. Potatoes are sourced from the Okara–Sahiwal–Kasur–Pakpattan belt in Punjab.
